# Client feedback and revisions

## Review cycle
1. Share a preview with the milestone, changed items, and specific areas needing review.
2. Ask one designated decision maker to collect feedback from other stakeholders.
3. Request feedback in one consolidated list, tied to a page or screenshot and expected behavior.
4. Triage each request as an in-scope defect, included revision, new request, or deferred item.
5. Record decisions in a GitHub issue; note scope or schedule impact before starting changed work.
6. Confirm what is ready for approval and close the milestone after agreed acceptance criteria are met.

## Working agreements
- Describe observable behavior and attach a screenshot when it helps.
- Keep credentials and private customer data out of issues and screenshots.
- Do not treat silence as approval unless the written agreement says so.
- Estimate and obtain written agreement for requests outside the approved scope.
