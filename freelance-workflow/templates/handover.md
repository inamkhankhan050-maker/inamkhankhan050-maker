# Website handover checklist

## Delivery
- [ ] Share the final public URL and identify the deployed release or commit.
- [ ] Confirm key pages, forms, links, and contact actions on the production site.
- [ ] Transfer or confirm client ownership of domain, hosting, repository, and third-party accounts.
- [ ] Invite the client's own accounts; do not send passwords in a README or issue.
- [ ] Document any environment variable names and where the owner manages them; never record values.
- [ ] Explain how to update text, images, services, and contact details.
- [ ] Share backup, restore, and rollback steps appropriate to the hosting platform.
- [ ] Provide source files and final approved assets in the agreed location.
- [ ] List known limitations, third-party dependencies, and recurring fees (if any).
- [ ] Confirm accessibility, browser, and device checks completed and note anything unverified.
- [ ] Record acceptance and close remaining issues or agree follow-up work.
