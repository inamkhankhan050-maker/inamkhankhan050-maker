# Client onboarding checklist

Use after agreeing to explore a project. Keep this checklist and any client-specific details in the client's private workspace.

## Before work starts
- [ ] Confirm the client contact and a backup contact for approvals.
- [ ] Collect the completed project requirements questionnaire.
- [ ] Confirm decision maker and response expectations.
- [ ] Agree written scope, deliverables, exclusions, revision rounds, and acceptance process.
- [ ] Confirm schedule, milestones, dependencies, and client review windows.
- [ ] Record the commercial agreement and payment schedule in the agreed private system.
- [ ] Confirm who owns the domain, hosting, content, design assets, and third-party accounts.
- [ ] Confirm how the client will provide copy, images, and approvals.

## Repository and access
- [ ] Create or identify the project repository; choose visibility appropriate to its contents.
- [ ] Invite collaborators by account, using the least access they need.
- [ ] Confirm the client retains ownership or administrative access to their domain and hosting.
- [ ] Use an approved password manager or host invitation flow for access; never commit or exchange passwords in issues.
- [ ] Store deployment secrets in the hosting provider's secret settings.
- [ ] Add a README and issue templates; create milestones and the agreed task board.

## Kickoff
- [ ] Confirm the first milestone and what counts as accepted.
- [ ] List missing client content and who will provide it.
- [ ] Record risks, dependencies, and decisions.
- [ ] Send a concise kickoff summary in the agreed communication channel.
