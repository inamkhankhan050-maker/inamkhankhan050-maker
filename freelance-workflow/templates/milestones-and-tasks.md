# Milestones and tasks

Use one milestone per meaningful phase or agreed delivery date. Keep issue titles actionable and small enough to review.

## Suggested phases
1. **Discovery and scope** — requirements, risks, deliverables, approval.
2. **Content and design** — content inventory, wireframe or visual direction, client approval.
3. **Build** — page structure, responsive styles, interactions, integrations.
4. **Review and QA** — client review, fixes, accessibility and device checks.
5. **Launch and handover** — deployment, smoke checks, access transfer, documentation.

## Issue checklist
Each task should include:
- Outcome and context
- Acceptance criteria that can be checked
- Owner
- Milestone
- Dependencies or client input
- Relevant label (`feature`, `bug`, `content`, `client-input`, or `launch`)

## Solo-friendly board
Use **Backlog → Ready → In progress → Waiting on client → Review → Done**. Keep only the next actionable work in **Ready**. Move blocked items to **Waiting on client** and state exactly what is needed and by when. Keep due dates limited to agreed commitments.
